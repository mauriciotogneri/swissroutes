# Swiss Routes

[Website](https://swiss-routes.web.app)

A map of Switzerland's official hiking, cycling and mountain bike routes, with places to stay, drinking water, bike service and more along the way.

## Features

- National, regional and local routes for hiking, cycling and mountain biking, each level turned on and off on its own
- Route filters by length, ascent, difficulty and fitness
- Route details: distance, ascent and descent, hiking time, start and end, an elevation chart, photos placed on the map, and a GPX download
- Places, each with its own toggle:
  - **Stay:** campsites, hostels, backpackers, B&Bs, group houses, farm stays, sleeping in straw, mountain huts and shelters, hotels, guest houses, chalets
  - **Explore:** sightseeing and ruins
  - **Water:** drinking water, springs, water points
  - **Bike:** service shops, e-bike charging, repair stations, air pumps, bike rental
  - **Rest:** picnic sites and tables, fire pits, toilets, showers
  - **Nature:** waterfalls, mountain passes, caves, swimming areas, bathing places
- Save routes and places to a personal list, kept in the browser
- Share links to a route or place, and to the current view with its layers and filters
- Show your own GPX track on the map
- Map, terrain and satellite views
- Light and dark themes
- In English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese

The site is made for desktop screens. Screens narrower than 1024 px see a notice instead of the map.
